01 Scope & Who We Are

This Privacy Policy explains how Legion V. Omni-Operation Holdings LLC, doing business as "LVO Holdings" or "LVO" ("we," "us," "our," or the "Company"), collects, uses, discloses, and safeguards information in connection with our websites, subdomains, applications, and related services (collectively, the "Services").

LVO Holdings is a privately held, Texas-based, multi-division operations group organized under the Marts Trust. This Policy applies to wearelvo.com and all of its subdomains and division portals, including but not limited to:

Vindex

Real estate operations, portfolio management, and related dashboards.

Alliance

Partnerships, capital formation, deal rooms, and investor-facing tools.

Operations

Social services and General Residential Operation (GRO) administration.

Careers

Recruiting, job postings, and applicant portals across LVO Holdings.

By accessing or using any part of the Services, you acknowledge that you have read and understood this Privacy Policy. If you do not agree with our practices, please do not use the Services. This Policy does not apply to information collected offline or through channels not owned or controlled by LVO Holdings, including third-party sites we may link to.

03 How We Collect It

We collect information when you: create or log into an account; submit a form (contact, application, deal inquiry, or investor interest); communicate with us by email, phone, or in-portal messaging; browse our Services, including through cookies and similar technologies; or when a third party (such as a partner, vendor, or public record source) provides information to us in connection with a legitimate business purpose described in this Policy.

04 How We Use Information

We use the information we collect for the following purposes:

  • Service Delivery: To create and maintain accounts, authenticate users, process applications, facilitate deal rooms, and operate division-specific portals.
  • Communications: To respond to inquiries, send transactional notices (e.g., verification emails, deal updates, application status), and, where you have opted in, marketing communications.
  • Operations & Administration: To manage real estate, partnership, staffing, and program operations across LVO Holdings' divisions, including compliance with GRO licensing obligations.
  • Security & Fraud Prevention: To detect, investigate, and prevent unauthorized access, fraud, or abuse of the Services.
  • Analytics & Improvement: To understand usage patterns, diagnose technical issues, and improve site performance, accessibility, and the motion-sensitivity experience described in Section 06.
  • Legal Compliance: To comply with applicable law, respond to lawful requests from public authorities, and enforce our Terms of Service.
  • Recruiting: To evaluate candidates for employment or contractor roles across LVO Holdings and its divisions, including licensed GRO staff.

We do not use information collected through the Services to make solely automated decisions that produce legal or similarly significant effects concerning you without human review.

05 Legal Bases for Processing

Where applicable law requires a legal basis for processing, we rely on one or more of the following: your consent; performance of a contract with you (e.g., an employment application, deal engagement, or account terms); our legitimate business interests (e.g., securing our Services, improving performance); and compliance with a legal obligation (e.g., HHSC licensing, tax, and employment-verification requirements).

07 How We Share Information

We do not sell your personal information for money. We may share information in the following circumstances:

  • Within LVO Holdings: Among LVO Holdings and its divisions (Vindex, Alliance, Operations, Careers), for the purposes described in this Policy.
  • Service Providers: With vendors who perform services on our behalf, such as hosting, identity verification, email delivery, and analytics, under contractual confidentiality and data-protection obligations. See Section 08.
  • Deal Counterparties: Within Alliance and Vindex, relevant deal or transaction information may be shared with counterparties, co-investors, lenders, or professional advisors strictly as needed to evaluate or complete a transaction, and generally only after you have expressed interest in that transaction.
  • Regulators & Licensing Bodies: With HHSC and other regulators as required for our GRO license and other licensing or compliance obligations.
  • Legal & Safety: Where required by law, subpoena, or court order, or where we believe disclosure is necessary to protect the rights, property, or safety of LVO Holdings, our users, or others.
  • Business Transfers: In connection with a merger, acquisition, financing, or sale of assets, subject to standard confidentiality protections.

08 Third-Party Service Providers

We rely on a limited set of infrastructure and service providers to operate the Services, including hosting and content-delivery (GitHub Pages and Cloudflare, including Cloudflare Workers and D1 database services), identity and authentication management, transactional email delivery (including SMTP-based sending), and, within certain internal tools, third-party large-language-model infrastructure used to power limited, deal-scoped assistant features inside the Alliance portal. Each provider is contractually restricted from using your information for purposes other than providing services to LVO Holdings, except as required by law.

09 Division-Specific Practices

Vindex (Real Estate)

Vindex may process property, portfolio, and counterparty information in connection with acquisitions, dispositions, and asset management. Public property records may be combined with information you provide for diligence purposes.

Alliance (Partnerships & Capital)

Alliance may collect accreditation, financial, and identity-verification information necessary to evaluate potential partnerships or capital arrangements. Nothing in this Policy, and no feature of the Alliance portal, constitutes an offer to sell or a solicitation of an offer to buy any security.

Operations (GRO)

LVO's Operations division operates a General Residential Operation licensed by the Texas Health and Human Services Commission (HHSC) under 26 TAC Chapter 748. Information relating to individuals in the program's care is subject to heightened confidentiality protections under applicable state licensing and child-welfare regulations, is accessed strictly on a need-to-know basis by authorized, background-checked personnel, and is not used for the marketing or analytics purposes described elsewhere in this Policy. Where state or federal program requirements impose stricter or additional obligations, those requirements govern over the general provisions of this Policy for that data.

Careers

Application materials submitted through the Careers portal are used solely for recruiting and hiring purposes across LVO Holdings and its divisions and are retained in accordance with Section 10.

10 Data Retention

We retain personal information for as long as necessary to fulfill the purposes described in this Policy, unless a longer retention period is required or permitted by law. As general guidance:

  • Account and authentication data is retained for the life of the account plus a limited period thereafter for security and audit purposes.
  • Application materials submitted via the Careers portal are retained for a reasonable evaluation period and, where permitted, a limited additional period for future opportunities, unless you request earlier deletion.
  • Transaction and deal-related records within Vindex and Alliance are retained in accordance with applicable financial, tax, and recordkeeping requirements.
  • GRO program records maintained by LVO's Operations division are retained in accordance with HHSC licensing and Texas child-welfare recordkeeping requirements, which may exceed the retention periods described elsewhere in this Policy.

11 Data Security

We implement administrative, technical, and physical safeguards designed to protect information against unauthorized access, alteration, disclosure, or destruction, including encryption in transit, access controls, credential hashing, and server-side token verification for authenticated portals. No method of transmission or storage is completely secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security. If we become aware of a security incident affecting your personal information, we will notify affected individuals and any applicable regulators as required by law.

12 Your Privacy Rights

Depending on your state of residence, you may have some or all of the following rights with respect to your personal information:

  • Right to Know / Access: Confirm whether we process your personal information and obtain a copy of it.
  • Right to Correct: Request correction of inaccurate personal information.
  • Right to Delete: Request deletion of personal information we hold about you, subject to legal exceptions.
  • Right to Data Portability: Receive a copy of your personal information in a portable format.
  • Right to Opt Out: Opt out of the processing of your personal information for targeted advertising, the sale of personal information, or profiling in furtherance of decisions that produce legal or similarly significant effects.
  • Right to Appeal: Appeal a decision we make regarding your rights request.

To exercise any of these rights, contact us using the details in Section 19. We will verify your request using information associated with your account or prior interactions with us before responding, and we will respond within the timeframe required by applicable law. You will not receive discriminatory treatment for exercising your privacy rights.

13 Texas Data Privacy & Security Act (TDPSA)

As a Texas-based company, LVO Holdings is committed to honoring the rights set out in the Texas Data Privacy and Security Act (TDPSA), effective July 1, 2024, to the extent it applies to our processing activities. In addition to the rights listed in Section 12, the TDPSA provides that:

  • We will not process sensitive data (such as precise geolocation, certain demographic characteristics, or health information) without your consent, except as otherwise permitted by law.
  • We provide a reasonably accessible, clear, and meaningful privacy notice — this Policy — describing the categories of personal data processed, the purposes of processing, and how to exercise your rights.
  • Where we engage in "targeted advertising," the "sale" of personal data, or certain profiling activities as those terms are defined by the TDPSA, you have the right to opt out. LVO Holdings does not currently sell personal data or engage in third-party targeted advertising through the Services; if this changes, we will update this Policy and provide an opt-out mechanism.
  • You may designate an authorized agent to submit a rights request on your behalf, subject to verification.

If you disagree with our response to a rights request, you may appeal by contacting us as described in Section 19. If your appeal is denied, you may contact the Texas Attorney General's office.

14 Do Not Track & Global Opt-Out Signals

Some browsers offer a "Do Not Track" signal. Because there is not yet a common industry standard for how to interpret this signal, our Services do not currently respond to Do Not Track signals. We will honor recognized universal opt-out mechanisms (such as the Global Privacy Control) where required by applicable law and where technically feasible for the associated processing activity.

15 Children's Privacy

The Services, including our corporate website, Vindex, Alliance, and Careers portals, are not directed to children under 13 (or the applicable minimum age in your jurisdiction), and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children through those Services. If we learn that we have collected personal information from a child in violation of this Policy, we will take steps to delete it.

This section does not apply to information collected through our licensed General Residential Operation program, which is governed by dedicated, program-specific confidentiality and consent protocols under HHSC regulations rather than by this general web Privacy Policy.

16 International Users & Data Transfers

The Services are hosted and operated in the United States and are intended primarily for a United States audience. If you access the Services from outside the United States, your information will be transferred to, stored, and processed in the United States, where data protection laws may differ from those of your jurisdiction.

17 Third-Party Links
