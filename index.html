We rely on trusted infrastructure providers to operate the Services:

These providers process data on our behalf under their own security and privacy commitments. We do not sell personal information to third parties, and we do not share it with advertisers.

5. Cookies & Similar Technologies

Our portals use essential cookies required for authentication (set by Clerk) and, where enabled, privacy-focused analytics (via Cloudflare) to understand aggregate site usage. We do not use third-party advertising trackers.

6. Data Retention

We retain account information for as long as your portal account remains active, plus a reasonable period afterward to comply with legal, accounting, or security obligations. You may request deletion of your account data as described in Section 7.

7. Your Rights

Depending on your location, you may have the right to access, correct, or request deletion of your personal information, or to object to certain processing. To exercise these rights, contact us using the details in Section 9. We will respond within a reasonable timeframe and may need to verify your identity before acting on a request.

8. Children's Privacy

The Services are not directed to individuals under 18, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children through these websites. If you believe a child has provided us with personal information, contact us and we will take appropriate steps to remove it.

9. Contact Us

Questions about this Privacy Policy, or requests regarding your data, can be sent to privacy@wearelvo.com.

We may update this Privacy Policy periodically. Material changes will be reflected by an updated "Last Updated" date above. We encourage you to review this page occasionally.
