01 Scope & Who We Are

This Privacy Policy explains how LVO Holdings LLC, doing business as Legion V. Omni-Operations Holdings ("LVO Holdings," "LVO," "we," "us," "our," or the "Company"), collects, uses, discloses, and safeguards information in connection with our websites, subdomains, applications, and related services (collectively, the "Services").

LVO Holdings is a privately held group. This Policy applies to lvoholdings.com, wearelvo.com, lvobscura.com, lvo-cloud.cloud, and all of their subdomains, division portals, and applications, including LVO Obscura (our private platform for sign-in, dashboards, mail, documents, chat, status reporting, and support; formerly referred to as "LVO Cloud"). The group is organized into three projects and ten divisions, including:

Obscura

IT, helpdesk, and the private LVO Obscura platform. Access is by invitation only.

Vindex

Real estate and vehicles: property portfolio management and related dashboards.

Alliance

Partnerships and deals, deal rooms, and partner-facing tools.

Operations

Social services. Planned General Residential Operation (GRO) care for children aged 5 to 13, subject to licensing.

Careers

Recruiting, job postings, and applicant portals across LVO Holdings.

Our other divisions — Capital, Custodia, Outreach & Restoration, Alatus, Equites, and Petra — are internal, support, or planned; see Section 9.

By accessing or using any part of the Services, you acknowledge that you have read and understood this Privacy Policy. If you do not agree with our practices, please do not use the Services. This Policy does not apply to information collected offline or through channels not owned or controlled by LVO Holdings, including third-party sites we may link to, or to employment or membership records that are governed by separate written terms.

03 How We Collect It

We collect information when you: create or log into an account; submit a form (application, deal inquiry, support ticket, or investor interest); send or receive mail or messages through LVO Obscura; upload a file or profile picture; communicate with us by email, phone, or in-portal messaging; browse our Services, including through cookies and similar technologies; or when a third party (such as a partner, vendor, or public record source) provides information to us in connection with a legitimate business purpose described in this Policy.

04 How We Use Information

We use the information we collect for the following purposes:

  • Service Delivery: To create and maintain accounts, authenticate users, deliver mail and messages, store documents, process applications and support tickets, facilitate deal rooms, and operate division-specific portals.
  • Communications: To respond to inquiries, send transactional notices (e.g., verification and password-reset emails, deal updates, application status), and, where you have opted in, other communications.
  • Operations & Administration: To manage real estate, partnership, staffing, and program operations across LVO Holdings' divisions, including compliance with licensing obligations.
  • Security, Oversight & Fraud Prevention: To detect, investigate, and prevent unauthorized access, abuse, or misuse; to limit repeated sign-in attempts; to maintain audit trails; and to review or redact content on LVO Obscura as described in Section 9.
  • AI & Translation Features: To generate assistant responses and translations you request.
  • Analytics & Improvement: To understand aggregate usage, diagnose technical issues, and improve site performance, accessibility, and the motion-sensitivity experience described in Section 6.
  • Legal Compliance: To comply with applicable law, respond to lawful requests from public authorities, and enforce our Terms of Service.
  • Recruiting: To evaluate candidates for employment or contractor roles across LVO Holdings and its divisions.

We do not use information collected through the Services to make solely automated decisions that produce legal or similarly significant effects concerning you without human review. AI assistant output is advisory only.

05 Legal Bases for Processing

Where applicable law requires a legal basis for processing, we rely on one or more of the following: your consent (for example, to optional cookies and live data layers); performance of a contract with you (e.g., an employment application, deal engagement, or account terms); our legitimate business interests (e.g., securing our Services, preventing abuse, improving performance); and compliance with a legal obligation (e.g., licensing, tax, and employment-verification requirements).

07 How We Share Information

We do not sell your personal information for money, and we do not share it for cross-context behavioral advertising. We may share information in the following circumstances:

  • Within LVO Holdings: Among LVO Holdings, its divisions, and affiliated entities of the group, for the purposes described in this Policy and on a need-to-know basis. Mail and messages are shared with the recipients and conversation members you choose.
  • Service Providers: With vendors who perform services on our behalf, such as hosting, email delivery, content delivery, and AI and translation. See Section 8.
  • Deal Counterparties: Within Alliance and Vindex, relevant deal or transaction information may be shared with counterparties, co-investors, lenders, or professional advisors strictly as needed to evaluate or complete a transaction, and generally only after you have expressed interest in that transaction.
  • Regulators & Licensing Bodies: With state licensing authorities and other regulators as required for any license or compliance obligation.
  • Legal & Safety: Where required by law, subpoena, or court order, or where we believe disclosure is necessary to protect the rights, property, or safety of LVO Holdings, our users, children in any LVO program, or others.
  • Business Transfers: In connection with a merger, acquisition, reorganization, financing, or sale of assets, subject to standard confidentiality protections.

09 Division-Specific Practices

Obscura (LVO Obscura Platform)

LVO Obscura is available only to authorized accounts. Messages, mail, tickets, and documents are stored on our Cloudflare infrastructure. Mail is visible to the account that owns the mailbox and to its recipients; chat messages and attachments are visible to the members of the channel or conversation they were posted in. Authorized LVO administrators may access or redact content on the platform — for example, through a communications oversight tool — and our legal team may access content for legal and compliance purposes. Administrative actions are recorded in the audit log. We do not use platform content for advertising.

AI assistants and translation. When you send a direct message to an AI assistant, or mention one in a channel, the assistant sends your message and a limited amount of recent conversation history to its AI provider to generate a reply. Administrators can configure an assistant to respond to every message in a channel it belongs to, in which case all messages in that channel are sent to the provider. When you use translation, the selected message text is sent to a translation service. Providers process this information under their own terms. Please do not enter resident, child, or program data, credentials, or other sensitive information into LVO Obscura or its assistants.

Status board. The status board reports the operational status of our services and does not require personal information to read. Its optional live layers load third-party data only after you consent (Section 6). For account recovery or support, contact support@wearelvo.com.

Vindex (Real Estate & Vehicles)

Vindex may process property, vehicle, portfolio, and counterparty information in connection with acquisitions, dispositions, and asset management. Public property records may be combined with information you provide for diligence purposes.

Alliance (Partnerships & Deals)

Alliance may collect accreditation, financial, and identity-verification information necessary to evaluate potential partnerships or deals. Nothing in this Policy, and no feature of the Alliance portal, constitutes an offer to sell or a solicitation of an offer to buy any security.

Operations (Residential Care for Youth)

LVO's Operations division is being set up to provide General Residential Operation care for children aged 5 to 13, subject to licensing by the applicable state authority and other approvals. The Services do not currently accept referrals or intake information about children. If and when a program operates, information relating to individuals in its care will be subject to heightened confidentiality protections under applicable state licensing and child-welfare regulations, accessed strictly on a need-to-know basis by authorized, background-checked personnel, and will not be used for the marketing or analytics purposes described elsewhere in this Policy. Where state or federal program requirements impose stricter or additional obligations, those requirements govern over this Policy for that data.

Careers

Application materials and account details submitted through the Careers portal are used for recruiting and hiring purposes across LVO Holdings and its divisions and are retained in accordance with Section 10. Authorized administrators can view, update, and delete Careers accounts and applications, and each such action is recorded in the audit log. Employment and membership records are governed by separate written terms.

Capital, Custodia, Alatus, Equites, Petra, and Outreach & Restoration

These divisions are internal, support, or planned. Capital handles internal treasury and does not offer financial products to the public. Where a division later collects personal information beyond what is described here — for example, facility camera and door-alert data (Custodia), drone imagery (Alatus), passenger or driver data (Equites), or donor and payment information (Outreach & Restoration, once nonprofit registration is in place) — we will update this Policy or provide a separate notice before that collection begins.

10 Data Retention

We retain personal information for as long as necessary to fulfill the purposes described in this Policy, unless a longer retention period is required or permitted by law. As general guidance:

  • Account and authentication data is retained for the life of the account plus a limited period thereafter for security and audit purposes. Session records expire automatically (after 8 hours or 7 days, depending on the account), and password-reset links are single-use and expire.
  • Audit logs are retained for as long as needed for security, accountability, and legal compliance.
  • Messaging content in LVO Chat is retained for up to one year, unless you request an export of your chats or a longer period is required by law or for a legal hold.
  • Mail, support tickets, and documents are retained while your account is active and for a limited period afterward, unless earlier deletion is requested and permitted. Documents you delete are first marked deleted and later removed.
  • Application materials and Careers accounts are retained for a reasonable evaluation period and, where permitted, a limited additional period for future opportunities, unless you request earlier deletion.
  • Transaction and deal-related records within Vindex and Alliance are retained in accordance with applicable financial, tax, and recordkeeping requirements.
  • Residential-program records, if and when an Operations program is licensed and operating, are retained in accordance with state licensing and child-welfare recordkeeping requirements, which may exceed the retention periods described elsewhere in this Policy.
  • Our account and careers databases are backed up on a weekly schedule to private cloud storage. Deleted information may persist in backups until those backups are rotated.

11 Data Security

We implement administrative, technical, and physical safeguards designed to protect information against unauthorized access, alteration, disclosure, or destruction, including encryption in transit (HTTPS with strict transport security), hashed passwords, two-step verification (password plus authenticator app), hashed session tokens that can be revoked, single-use sign-in challenges and replay protection for authenticator codes, limits on repeated failed sign-ins, role-based and division-based access controls, audit logging, security response headers, and regular backups. We publish a security contact at security@wearelvo.com, and we welcome good-faith vulnerability reports sent to that address. No method of transmission or storage is completely secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security. Email and chat are not recommended channels for sensitive personal information. If we become aware of a security incident affecting your personal information, we will notify affected individuals and any applicable regulators as required by law.

12 Your Privacy Rights

Depending on your state of residence, you may have some or all of the following rights with respect to your personal information:

  • Right to Know / Access: Confirm whether we process your personal information and obtain a copy of it.
  • Right to Correct: Request correction of inaccurate personal information.
  • Right to Delete: Request deletion of personal information we hold about you, subject to legal exceptions.
  • Right to Data Portability: Receive a copy of your personal information in a portable format.
  • Right to Opt Out: Opt out of the processing of your personal information for targeted advertising, the sale of personal information, or profiling in furtherance of decisions that produce legal or similarly significant effects.
  • Right to Withdraw Consent: Where we rely on your consent (for example, to optional cookies and live data layers), you may withdraw it at any time through our cookie settings.
  • Right to Appeal: Appeal a decision we make regarding your rights request.

To exercise any of these rights, contact us using the details in Section 19. We will verify your request using information associated with your account or prior interactions with us before responding, and we will respond within the timeframe required by applicable law. You will not receive discriminatory treatment for exercising your privacy rights. Some information, such as audit logs and records we must keep by law, may be retained despite a deletion request.

13 State Privacy Laws

LVO Holdings is committed to honoring the rights set out in applicable U.S. state consumer privacy laws, to the extent they apply to our processing activities. In addition to the rights listed in Section 12:

  • We will not process sensitive data (such as precise geolocation, certain demographic characteristics, or health information) without your consent, except as otherwise permitted by law.
  • We provide a reasonably accessible, clear, and meaningful privacy notice — this Policy — describing the categories of personal data processed, the purposes of processing, and how to exercise your rights.
  • Where we engage in “targeted advertising,” the “sale” of personal data, or certain profiling activities as those terms are defined by applicable law, you have the right to opt out. LVO Holdings does not currently sell personal data or engage in third-party targeted advertising through the Services; if this changes, we will update this Policy and provide an opt-out mechanism.
  • You may designate an authorized agent to submit a rights request on your behalf, subject to verification.
  • If you disagree with our response to a rights request, you may appeal by contacting us as described in Section 19. If your appeal is denied, you may contact your state attorney general's office.

14 Do Not Track & Global Opt-Out Signals

Some browsers offer a “Do Not Track” signal. Because there is not yet a common industry standard for how to interpret this signal, our Services do not currently respond to Do Not Track signals. We honor the Global Privacy Control (GPC) signal on LVO Obscura and our division portals by treating it as an “Essential only” choice, so optional third-party features stay off, and we will honor other recognized universal opt-out mechanisms where required by applicable law and where technically feasible.

15 Children's Privacy

The Services, including lvoholdings.com, wearelvo.com, LVO Obscura, and the Vindex, Alliance, and Careers portals, are not directed to children under 13 (or the applicable minimum age in your jurisdiction), accounts are for adults, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children through those Services. Please do not send us a child's personal information through the contact page or any other channel. If we learn that we have collected personal information from a child in violation of this Policy, we will take steps to delete it.

This section does not apply to information that may be collected through a licensed General Residential Operation program once one is operating, which will be governed by dedicated, program-specific confidentiality and consent protocols under state regulations rather than by this general web Privacy Policy.

16 International Users & Data Transfers

The Services are hosted and operated in the United States, including on Cloudflare's global network, and are intended primarily for a United States audience. Our public site is offered in several languages and regions for convenience, and LVO members live in many countries. If you access the Services from outside the United States, your information will be transferred to, stored, and processed in the United States and by our providers in other countries, where data protection laws may differ from those of your jurisdiction. Where applicable law requires safeguards for such transfers, we will put them in place.
