The Framework
01

Our Privacy Philosophy

LVO Holdings (Legion V · Omni-Operations) is a Houston-based organization dedicated to real estate acquisition, social infrastructure, and strategic capital ventures. Our digital presence at wearelvo.com is an informational gateway designed for private correspondence and the evaluation of strategic partnerships. This Policy applies to that gateway and its subdomains; it does not apply to the separately governed client and program records of our licensed operating divisions.

We do not engage in public-facing advertising or e-commerce. We do not collect payment card information, billing addresses, or consumer shopping histories. Access is earned, not given — and the same principle governs your data.

02

Information We Collect

We limit our data collection to what is adequate, relevant, and reasonably necessary for our disclosed operations.

Voluntary Inquiries: When you contact us via our Private Inquiries email or external connection portals, we collect the personal information you voluntarily provide — such as your name, business affiliation, and contact details.

Administrative Log Data: Our hosting and security provider automatically collects basic metadata, such as IP addresses, browser type, and access timestamps, to ensure site security and functionality.

04

Use of Your Personal Information

We process your information exclusively to facilitate the earned access and private correspondence models of our organization. This includes evaluating potential partnerships within our three pillars:

Vindex · Real Estate  ·  Operations · Social Services  ·  Alliance · Strategic Partnerships

We do not use your personal information for targeted advertising or automated profiling that produces legal or similarly significant effects.

Data & Rights
05

No Sale of Personal Data

LVO Holdings does not sell your personal data. We define "sale" as the sharing or transferring of personal data to a third party for monetary or other valuable consideration. We only disclose information to:

Vetted Processors: Service providers who assist in our technical operations (e.g., email hosting, site infrastructure) and are contractually bound to maintain your data's confidentiality.

Legal Mandates: Authorities where disclosure is required to comply with a legal proceeding or protect the legal rights, safety, or property of LVO Holdings or others.

Corporate Transactions: A successor entity in connection with a merger, acquisition, or sale of assets, subject to this Policy or a materially equivalent one.

06

Data Retention

We retain personal information collected through voluntary inquiries only as long as reasonably necessary to evaluate and respond to that inquiry, maintain business records, or comply with legal, accounting, or reporting obligations. Administrative log data is retained for a limited period consistent with our security and hosting provider's standard practices, after which it is deleted or aggregated.

07

Sensitive Data & Minors

We do not process sensitive personal data — such as health, ethnic, or religious information — or biometric data without your prior, affirmative consent. This gateway Site is intended for business and professional correspondence and is not directed to individuals under 18. We do not knowingly collect personal data from minors through this Site; if we learn we have done so, we will delete it. This is separate from, and does not limit, the heightened confidentiality protections that apply to program participants under our licensed social-services operations.

08

Your Texas Privacy Rights

Under the Texas Data Privacy and Security Act (TDPSA), Texas residents have the following rights regarding their personal data:

  • Right of Access: To confirm if we are processing your data and to obtain a copy in a portable format.
  • Right of Correction: To rectify inaccuracies in the personal data we hold about you.
  • Right of Deletion: To request that we delete data you have provided or that we have obtained about you.
  • Right to Opt-Out: To opt-out of the processing of your data for sales, targeted advertising, or certain profiling, should such activities ever occur.
  • Right of Non-Discrimination: We will not deny you goods or services, charge different prices, or provide a different level of service because you exercised a privacy right.
09

Universal Opt-Out Mechanisms

Because we do not sell personal data or serve targeted advertising, there is currently no opt-out process to activate. If that changes, we will honor legally recognized universal opt-out signals (such as the Global Privacy Control) from browsers that support them, in accordance with the TDPSA.

10

Contact & Requests

To exercise these rights, please contact us at our protected inquiry address. We will authenticate your request and respond within 45 days, with one 45-day extension where reasonably necessary. If a request is denied, you have the right to appeal via the same address within a reasonable time; if the appeal is unsuccessful, you may submit a complaint to the Texas Attorney General.
